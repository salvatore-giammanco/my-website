---
title: When the world goes quiet
date: 2026-07-26
---

For months, maybe years, I've been wondering why I can't seem to relax. Over time I've learned that my brain, being AuDHD, doesn't relax in conventional ways. Some people suggest doing no-brainer, repetitive activities—preferably hands-on.

The truth, though, is that no matter what I try, that feeling of never truly being able to unplug remains.

It remains until the world goes quiet. It’s one in the morning on a July Saturday, I’ve just finished watching Ghost in the Shell, and I’m on the balcony, fortunate enough to still enjoy the cool breeze of a summer night. Extreme heatwaves are becoming more frequent with each passing year.

In these moments of peace and quiet, while the bustling life of the city takes a break, I am free. The streetlights aren't enough to overload my stimulus-sensitive brain. The streets are empty, the houses silent. Finally, the people are gone—those who, with their huge, invisible sphere of influence, crush me against myself.

Lost in thought, I suddenly realize: that relentless, invincible feeling simply isn't there anymore.

This moment is a gift, a blessing from a generous night that grants me a glimpse of peace in its purest form.

I am free... only when the world goes quiet.
