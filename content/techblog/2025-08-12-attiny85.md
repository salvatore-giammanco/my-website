## ATtiny85

As its name says, it's a tiny ATMega microcontroller. With 6 digital pins, of which 3 are also analogic and 2 are PWM capable, an input voltage range that goes from 2.7 to 5.5V, it's out to become my favourite microcontroller. You can read the full specs on [microchip.com](https://www.microchip.com/en-us/product/attiny85).
